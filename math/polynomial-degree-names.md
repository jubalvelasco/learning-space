# **Polynomial Degree Names**

- **Special case** – the zero polynomial (its degree is either left undefined, or defined as $-1$ or $-\infty$)
- **Degree 0** – non-zero constant
- **Degree 1** – linear
- **Degree 2** – quadratic
- **Degree 3** – cubic
- **Degree 4** – quartic (or, if all terms have even degree, biquadratic)
- **Degree 5** – quintic
- **Degree 6** – sextic (or, less commonly, hexic)
- **Degree 7** – septic (or, less commonly, heptic)
- **Degree 8** – octic
- **Degree 9** – nonic
- **Degree 10** – decic
